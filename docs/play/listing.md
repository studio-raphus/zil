## Full description (EN)
Explore Mauritius the fun way. Zil puts the whole island on a playful 3D map, with the
places locals love and what's happening right now.

• Beaches, hikes, food spots and culture, hand-picked by the Zil team
• Live now: queues, boat times, sunsets and busy spots, updated through the day
• Events: night markets, festivals and live music for the week ahead
• Directions by road from where you are, with drive times
• Street map for the detail, which you can save to use offline on beaches and hikes
• Drop your own secret spots (private until our team checks them)
• Day and night map, in English and French

Sign in with Google to keep your account. We only keep your name and email.
